# Appendix A — Every menu

**Language:** English · [ภาษาไทย](../th/A-menus.md)

> Generated from the app itself, version **2.0.0-alpha.169** (English · default settings). Menu names and shortcuts on your machine may differ with the language you chose and the shortcuts you rebound.
> On macOS: `Ctrl` = `⌘` · `Alt` = `⌥`

[← Back to the manual contents](README.md)

The menu bar sits at the top of the window (on macOS it is in the system menu bar). Tap `Alt` or press `F10` to enter the menu bar from the keyboard, then `←` `→` to move, `Enter`/`↓` to open, `Esc` to leave.

Items ending in `…` open a dialog. Some items in the **Script** menu are only enabled while the open tab is a screenplay.
